## 🌷 About Me

I'm a Computer Science & Design Studies student at **DePauw University** (Class of 2028), blending clean code with intentional design. I care as much about how something *looks* as how it *works*.

- 🎨 Passionate about **web design & development** — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React
- 📸 Also love **photography** — Adobe Lightroom, composition, editing
- 👩‍💻 Currently learning cybersecurity fundamentals, software engineering, and graphic design
- 🌱 President of **Adventure Club** & Vice President of **Women in Computer Science (WiCS)** at DePauw
- 💼 Marketing & Web Development Intern @ The Hubbard Center for Student Engagement
- 🖥️ IT Associate @ Tenzer Technology Center
